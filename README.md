A short, funny rant about a small everyday annoyance — the office printer —
written for the [Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046) on make.fun. A furious judge model reads the rants two at a time and picks the one that lands hardest.

## The rant

> The office printer has one job and it has chosen violence. It jams only on the page I need, prints my boarding pass in a font last seen on a ransom note, and blinks PC LOAD LETTER like a threat. I have fed it paper, ink, and the best years of my career. It has repaid me in paper cuts and contempt.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the rant to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–400 characters, per the competition gate).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
